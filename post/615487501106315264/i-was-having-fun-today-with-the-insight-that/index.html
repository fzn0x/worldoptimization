I was having fun today with the insight that relationships can be momentumy or mean-reverty (or both, on different timescales), and classifying my relationships as such

mean-reverty:

  • they say something I find hurtful. I act hurt until they notice and start being extra nice to make up for it, at which point I go back to normal
  • they’re uncomfortable with intimacy so whenever we start to get too close they pull away. if I give them space they come back

momentumy:

  • they do something considerate for me. I’m touched and do something considerate back. the virtuous cycle continues
  • they do something I think is bad, ignoring my protests. I’m pissed so I do something I know they think is bad, etc. the vicious cycle continues

I haven’t decided which … is better … idk, mean reversion seems like it will always be a bit disappointing. Otoh, even if you’re in the good phase of a momentumy relationship it’s kinda terrifying because stuff can spiral into a shitshow real quick.
